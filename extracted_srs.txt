1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification defines the complete functional, technical, operational, security, and business requirements for the BOLDDEE RENTALS Web Application.

The system will provide a centralized digital platform through which customers can:

Browse graduation and event products

Purchase personalized graduation items

Rent event equipment

Book tents, chairs, tables and related services

Request photography and videography services

Request event decoration

Create customized graduation/class orders

Request quotations for large events

Make online payments

Schedule delivery or pickup

Track orders and rental bookings

Receive notifications

Review completed services

Administrators and authorized staff will use a secure backend to:

Manage products

Manage rental inventory

Manage customers

Process orders

Approve customized designs

Manage bookings

Manage payments

Manage deliveries

Manage vendors/service partners

Manage graduation campaigns

Manage pricing

Manage promotional offers

Monitor business performance

Generate reports

Manage website content

The application should initially position BOLDDEE RENTALS as a graduation-focused event services company, while the underlying architecture must allow the company to expand into the broader event-rental market.

2. BUSINESS VISION

2.1 Vision Statement

To become a trusted Ghanaian digital platform for graduation celebrations and event rentals by giving customers one convenient place to obtain products, equipment, services, and event support.

2.2 Brand Promise

BOLDDEE RENTALS

We Make Your Moments Worthwhile.

2.3 Business Concept

The platform will operate around four primary commercial areas:

A. BOLDDEE GRAD

Graduation products and packages.

B. BOLDDEE RENTALS

Tents, chairs, tables, lighting, sound equipment, décor structures, and other event equipment.

C. BOLDDEE MOMENTS

Photography, videography, photo booths and memory services.

D. BOLDDEE EVENTS

Event setup, decoration, coordination, catering partnerships and other event services.

3. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Graduating students and event organizers typically have to contact several vendors separately.

A graduate may need to find:

A sash vendor

A medal supplier

A photographer

A tent provider

A chair/table provider

A decorator

A sound provider

A gift provider

This creates several problems:

Fragmented purchasing

Unclear pricing

Difficulty comparing services

Manual communication

Poor booking coordination

Payment uncertainty

Difficulty tracking orders

Lack of centralized customer records

Increased risk of missed delivery dates

Limited visibility for the business owner

BOLDDEE RENTALS intends to solve this by providing a unified digital platform.

11. BUSINESS RULES

BR-001: Booking Availability

The system must never confirm a rental booking if required inventory is unavailable.

BR-002: Payment Confirmation

An order requiring payment must not be marked fully confirmed until the payment gateway confirms payment.

BR-003: Personalized Production

Personalized products requiring approval should not move to production before customer approval.

BR-004: Balance Payment

For bookings requiring a balance, the system must clearly show the outstanding amount and deadline.

BR-005: Cancellation

Cancellation rules must depend on:

Product type

Rental type

Time remaining

Production status

Personalized goods may have different cancellation rules.

BR-006: Security Deposits

Security deposits must be tracked separately from revenue.

BR-007: Rental Completion

A rental booking cannot be completed until the equipment is returned or otherwise accounted for.

BR-008: Inventory Update

Inventory availability must automatically update when bookings change.

BR-009: Double Booking Protection

Concurrent requests for limited rental assets must be handled transactionally to prevent overbooking.

57. CRITICAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The system should not be designed as merely a website that says:

"Call us to book."

The objective is to transform the business into a transactional digital platform where the customer can complete most of the process without manual intervention.

The platform should therefore follow this principle:

DISCOVER → CONFIGURE → BOOK/BUY → PAY → TRACK → RECEIVE → REVIEW

The BOLDDEE RENTALS Web Application is a graduation and event commerce/rental platform designed initially for Ghanaian university graduates and their families.

Final-year students and graduating cohorts.

The Ghanaian events and equipment-rental industry.
